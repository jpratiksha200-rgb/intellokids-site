Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short

  • We collect only what IntelloKids needs: a parent's name and email, and each child's first name or nickname, age and learning progress.
  • Your family's account and progress are stored securely on servers in the European Union. Messages your child writes to Brainy stay on your device.
  • We never sell data, never show ads to children and never build advertising profiles.
  • Payments on our website are handled by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.
  • You can see, download or delete your family's data at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Intellokids Ltd is the data controller: the company responsible for your information. For any privacy question or request, email help@intellokids.com.

2. What we collect

  • About you (the parent): name, email address, password (stored only in protected, non-readable form), the 4-digit parent PIN that keeps each child in their own playground (we keep only a coded version of it, never the digits; a PIN is short, so it is a lock for children, not a password), language, the date your free trial started and, if you subscribe: your plan, start date, your card's brand and last 4 digits as Paddle reports them, and Paddle's reference numbers for the purchase.
  • About your child: a first name or nickname, and age. We recommend using a nickname.
  • Learning progress: activities played, levels, stars, correct answers, mistakes, time spent, stickers, colouring and videos watched, so we can show you progress and adapt difficulty.
  • Characters: the look your child chooses for themselves and for their virtual friend, and the friend's name.
  • Messages to Brainy: if your child uses Talk to Brainy, the messages they type and Brainy's answers, so you can read them. They stay on your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else. Personal details a child types (such as a phone number) are hidden automatically. You can delete them or switch the chat off at any time.
  • Technical information: basic device and browser information needed to run the service securely.
  • Feedback you choose to send: only what you type in our feedback form, when you press Send (see section 11).
  • Account emails: we send you the messages your account needs (confirming your email address, resetting your password, security notices such as "your password was changed").
  • Trial record: to give each family one free trial, we keep a coded fingerprint of your email address, which cannot be turned back into the address, for 24 months, also after you delete your account.
  • Country: the country your sign-up came from, worked out from the connection. We never store IP addresses.
  • Activity counts: the days your family signs in and plays, and how many games are finished, so we can show you progress and understand how IntelloKids is used. Our own statistics are anonymous daily totals (for example "12 families signed up today") that say nothing about any family or child.
  • Visits: we count visits to our website and app anonymously and without cookies: the page, the language, the country (worked out from the connection, not stored as an address) and the website you came from (its name only). To count each visitor once a day, your browser keeps a random code for that day only, and we keep a coded version of it for no more than 2 days; it is not sent if your browser asks not to be tracked. We use no third-party analytics.

We do not collect children's photos, voice recordings, precise location, contacts, and we do not receive the messages children write to Brainy.

3. Payments

On our website, payments are processed by Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited), our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects and processes your payment details (such as your card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay details), billing address and tax information as an independent controller under its own Privacy Policy. We never see or store your full card number or security code. We only keep the card type and its last 4 digits (for example Visa •••• 4242), so you can recognise it in Settings → Subscription.

  • When you open the checkout, we send Paddle the email address of your IntelloKids account (to fill in the checkout and link the payment to your account) and the number of children on it. We never send your children's names, ages or progress.
  • Paddle tells us only what we need to manage your subscription, such as the plan, the payment status and the reference numbers of the purchase. Paddle also has your email, country and payment details, because it is the seller.
  • Paddle's checkout and its price display are loaded from Paddle's servers only on the parents' plans screen (behind your password) and on Paddle payment links, never in the children's area and never in our Android app.

4. How we use information

  • To provide IntelloKids: save progress, unlock levels, grow the virtual friend and choose age-appropriate content.
  • To show you how your child is learning.
  • To manage your account and subscription and send important service messages.
  • To keep the service safe and secure.

We never sell or rent personal information, show advertising to children, or use children's information for marketing.

5. Who we share it with

Only trusted providers that help us run IntelloKids, under agreements that protect your information: Cloudflare (our servers and secure database in the European Union, and sending account emails), Paddle (payments and subscriptions, see section 3), Google Play (payments in our Android app, when they open) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect a child's safety.

Your data is stored in the European Union. Where a provider processes it elsewhere, we use safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Security

How we store and protect your family's information is explained on our Data Security page. If a security incident affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

7. How long we keep it

We keep information while your account is open. Sign-ups whose email address is never confirmed are deleted after 30 days. If you ask to change your email address but never confirm the new one, we forget it when the link expires. Deleting a child's profile deletes their progress at once. If you close your account, we delete your and your children's information at once, cancel your website subscription and remove your saved card from Paddle. Afterwards we keep only: the trial record described in section 2 (24 months); for 30 days, a record of your past subscription (its plan, dates and country, with nothing that identifies you); and anonymous daily totals that say nothing about any family or child. While your account is open, the days-active record is kept for up to 400 days. Paddle, as the seller, keeps its own billing records as the law requires. Anything we delete is also gone from our backups within 30 days. Security records (counts of sign-in attempts and other requests) are deleted within 2 days.

8. Your rights

  • See, correct or download the information we hold about you and your child.
  • Ask us to delete it, or delete a child's profile yourself in the Parents' area.
  • Download all your data, and delete your whole account, yourself in Settings (your password is needed).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time; we will then stop collecting your child's information and delete it.
  • Parents in the US: under COPPA you can review your child's information, have it deleted and refuse further collection.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email help@intellokids.com. We respond within 30 days. For payment data held by Paddle, you can also contact Paddle as described in its Privacy Policy.

9. Children's privacy and parental consent

We collect a child's information only with a parent's consent, given in these steps:

  • An adult parent account. Only a parent or legal guardian aged 18 or over can sign up, and accepts these terms when doing so.
  • A confirmed email. Nothing about a child is stored on our servers until the parent has confirmed their email address.
  • Consent for each child. When adding a child, the parent ticks a box confirming they are that child's parent or legal guardian and agree to us keeping the child's first name, age and learning progress to run their account. We record the date and the version of this policy. A child is not added without it.
  • A notice by email. When the first child is added, we email the parent a short summary of what we keep about the child and how to remove it.

You can withdraw your consent at any time: remove the child in the Parents' area, delete your account in Settings, or email help@intellokids.com. Removing a child deletes their profile and progress.

We aim to follow children's privacy laws, including the GDPR and UK GDPR, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

10. Changes

We will tell you about important changes to this policy by email or in the app before they apply.

11. The feedback form

The feedback card at the bottom of the Parents' area sends your star rating and your message, with the site language. We keep it in our secure database in the European Union and email it to the IntelloKids team, with your account name and email so we can reply. No other service receives it. We use it only to improve IntelloKids and to reply to you. Nothing about your children is sent, and nothing is sent until you press Send. It is deleted when you delete your account. The copy emailed to our team can be deleted on request: just email us.
