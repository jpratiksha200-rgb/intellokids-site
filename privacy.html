Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short

  • We collect only what IntelloKids needs: a parent's name and email, and each child's first name or nickname, age and learning progress.
  • We never sell data, never show ads to children and never build advertising profiles.
  • You can see, download or delete your family's data at any time.

1. Who is responsible

The IntelloKids team is responsible for your information. For any privacy question or request, email kidsintello@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • About you (the parent): name, email address, password (stored only in protected, non-readable form), and language.
  • About your child: a first name or nickname, and age. We recommend using a nickname.
  • Learning progress: activities played, levels, stars, correct answers, mistakes, time spent, stickers, colouring and videos watched, so we can show you progress and adapt difficulty.
  • Characters: the look your child chooses for themselves and for their virtual friend, and the friend's name.
  • Messages to Brainy: if your child uses Talk to Brainy, the messages they type and Brainy's answers, so you can read them. They stay on your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else. Personal details a child types (such as a phone number) are hidden automatically. You can delete them or switch the chat off at any time.
  • Technical information: basic device and browser information needed to run the service securely.
  • Feedback you choose to send: only what you type in our feedback form, when you press Send (see section 10).

We do not collect children's photos, voice recordings, precise location, contacts, and we do not receive the messages children write to Brainy.

3. How we use information

  • To provide IntelloKids: save progress, unlock levels, grow the virtual friend and choose age-appropriate content.
  • To show you how your child is learning.
  • To manage your account and send important service messages.
  • To keep the service safe and secure.

We never sell or rent personal information, show advertising to children, or use children's information for marketing.

4. Who we share it with

Only trusted providers that help us run IntelloKids, under agreements that protect your information: GitHub Pages (website hosting), FormSubmit (delivers the feedback form to our email) and, when cloud accounts launch, Amazon Web Services (secure data storage). We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect a child's safety.

5. Security

How we store and protect your family's information is explained on our Data Security page. If a security incident affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep information while your account is open. Deleting a child's profile deletes their progress. If you close your account, we delete your and your children's information within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.

7. Your rights

  • See, correct or download the information we hold about you and your child.
  • Ask us to delete it, or delete a child's profile yourself in the Parents' area.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time; we will then stop collecting your child's information and delete it.
  • Parents in the US: under COPPA you can review your child's information, have it deleted and refuse further collection.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email kidsintello@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy laws

IntelloKids is designed to comply with children's privacy laws, including the GDPR and UK GDPR, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We collect children's information only with a parent's consent.

9. Changes

We will tell you about important changes to this policy by email or in the app before they apply.

10. The feedback form

Our feedback form sends what you type (your star rating, your message, and your name and email if you add them), with the language and the page you were on, to the IntelloKids email inbox through the FormSubmit service. We use it only to improve IntelloKids and to reply to you. Nothing about your children is sent, and nothing is sent until you press Send. To have a message deleted, email us.
