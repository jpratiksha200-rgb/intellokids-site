Data Security

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Protecting children's information is at the heart of IntelloKids. This page explains, in plain language, how we keep your family's data safe today and what we commit to as the service grows.

1. Where your data is stored today

IntelloKids is in early access. Your parent account, your children's profiles and their progress are stored only on your own device, in your browser's secure local storage. They are not sent to or kept on our servers. This means:

  • No one else, including us, can see your children's data.
  • Clearing your browser data, or using another device, starts afresh.
  • On a shared computer, log out when you finish.

2. Our commitments as we launch cloud accounts

To let families use IntelloKids across devices, we are building secure cloud accounts on Amazon Web Services. We will update this page and tell you before any data is moved.

  • Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Passwords stored only as salted, slow hashes, never in readable form.
  • Access limited to authorised people, protected by multi-factor authentication and least-privilege permissions, and logged.
  • Regular backups, and deletion within 30 days when you close your account.

3. Safe by design

  • We collect the minimum: a first name or nickname, an age and learning progress.
  • No advertising, no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics.
  • No chat with other people, no public profiles and no contact between users. Messages to Brainy, our helper, stay on your device.
  • No links out of the children's area.
  • The website is served only over HTTPS, with fonts hosted on our own site.

4. If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects your information, we will tell you without undue delay, explain what happened and what to do, and notify the authorities as the law requires (for example within 72 hours under the GDPR).

5. Your controls

  • Delete a child's profile at any time in the Parents' area; their progress is deleted with it.
  • Ask us to see, download or delete your information (see our Privacy Policy).

6. Report a security concern

Please email kidsintello@gmail.com. We treat every report seriously and respond promptly.
