Everything your child needs to learn and have fun
  • 👫

    A virtual friend

    Your child creates a friend who grows up alongside them, with Brainy always by their side.

    100 levels across 10 colourful worlds, from the clouds to outer space. A little airplane flies along as your child levels up, and every 5 levels a treasure chest opens a fun bonus game made for their age.

    Mini games

    17 games for shapes, colours, counting, sums, memory, patterns, logic and coding, getting harder as your child grows.

    A sticker album for every age: over 180 collectible stickers, from farm animals for 3-year-olds to dragons, princesses and pixel art for older children. Won through fun learning challenges, with a gift-box surprise for every win.

    Short, mixed quizzes made for each age, and a big quiz at the end of every world.

  • 🔊

    English, French and Spanish, with more languages coming soon. Brainy reads every instruction aloud, so children who cannot read yet can still play.

  • 🎬

    Learning videos

    Sing-along learning videos with Brainy: shapes, colours, counting to 10, the ABC, animals and their homes, and our solar system, spoken and sung in English, French and Spanish. More coming soon.

Meet Brainy!

Brainy is our clever, cheerful guide. Brainy cheers your child on, gives hints and celebrates every win. From age 6, children can also talk to Brainy for help with sums, spelling and curious questions, safely: no strangers, and parents can see every chat.

Questions parents ask

What is IntelloKids?

IntelloKids is a safe online learning world for children aged 3 to 10. Kids learn shapes, colours, counting, maths, logic and early coding through educational games, easy quizzes, colouring and a 100-level adventure map, guided by Brainy and a virtual friend who grows up with them.

What ages is IntelloKids for?

Children aged 3 to 10. Activities adapt to three age groups: Little Learners (3–4), Junior Thinkers (5–6) and Young Explorers (7–10), and get harder level by level.

Is IntelloKids safe for my child?

Yes. There are no ads, no chat with other people and no public profiles. Children aged 6 and over can talk to Brainy, our safe helper: its answers are made on your device, personal details are hidden automatically, and parents can read every conversation or switch it off. Only parents can create an account, and we collect as little information about children as possible. Read our Terms & Privacy for details.

Can I follow my child’s progress?

Yes. The parents’ dashboard shows time played, correct answers, answer speed, the mistakes your child makes most often, and the activities where they need more practice.

Which languages are available?

IntelloKids is available in English, French and Spanish, with more languages coming soon. Brainy reads instructions aloud, so children who cannot read yet can still play.

Does IntelloKids work on phones and tablets?

Yes. IntelloKids works in any modern browser on computers, tablets and phones. An Android app is coming soon.

What will my child learn?

Shapes, colours, counting, comparing sizes, addition and early maths, memory, patterns, logic and first steps in coding, plus creativity through colouring. Each age group gets its own mix, and the difficulty rises as your child progresses.
