Questions parents ask

What is IntelloKids?

A safe online learning platform for children aged 3 to 10: learning games, short videos, colouring, quizzes and a 100-level adventure with Brainy. Parents follow progress in their own dashboard.

Is IntelloKids free?

You can try everything free for 7 days, no card needed. After that, the free part stays open and nothing is charged. A family plan unlocks everything; it renews each month or year until you cancel, and you can cancel anytime in Settings → Subscription.

What ages is IntelloKids for?

Children aged 3 to 10, in three groups: Little Learners (3–4), Junior Thinkers (5–6) and Young Explorers (7–10).

Is IntelloKids safe for my child?

Yes. There are no ads, no chat with other people and no public profiles, and only parents can create an account. From age 6, children can talk to Brainy: its answers are made on your device, and parents can read every chat or switch it off. A parent PIN keeps each child in their own playground.

Is there an IntelloKids app for phones and tablets?

IntelloKids works in any modern browser on computers, tablets and phones, with nothing to install. An Android app is coming soon.

Which languages are available?

English, French and Spanish. Brainy reads instructions aloud, so children who can’t read yet can play.
